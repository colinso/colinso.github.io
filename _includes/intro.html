👨‍💻 Career

I'm skilled in backend development and system design, with a focus on security and scalability. I hold a B.S. in Computer Engineering, with a minor in Computer Science, but gave up on low-level programming/hardware pretty much as soon as I was able. It's cool, but not my thing. I did learn a lot, however, about working under tight system constraints, as well as how operating systems and embedded systems work, which has given me a deep appreciation for infrastructure work.

In my professional experience, I have designed and implemented varying business-critical projects, including several authentication systems, high-volume email ingestion platforms, and robust billing systems. My passion especially lies in authentication systems, which I love due to their complex and highly sensitive nature. There's nothing quite like a fun problem with actual stakes! In addition to my technical skills, I am also a talented mentor and trainer, having led team and department level trainings on web authentication methods and mentored junior engineers on application development and system design.

As my career continues, I hope to continue finding opportunities to grow and contribute. I got into software because I love the endless potential for creativity. It never quite gets credit as an art, but to me it is, and it's one I intend to master.
